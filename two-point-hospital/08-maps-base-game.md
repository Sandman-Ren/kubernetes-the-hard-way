# 08 — Base Game Maps: Per-Hospital Strategy (Levels 1–15 + R.E.M.I.X)

Covers the 15 career hospitals of the Two Point Hospital base game in career order, plus their
R.E.M.I.X variants. R.E.M.I.X mode (free update, Feb/May 2020) exists **only for the first six
hospitals** — Hogsport, Lower Bullocks, Flottering (unlocked at 1 star in Flottering), and
Mitton University, Tumble, Flemington (unlocked at 1 star in Flemington). Levels 7–15 have no
remix. Each remix awards one blue star, $10,000 and 500 Kudosh, plus a randomly generated
"S for S-word" rank letter.

## Level 1: Hogsport

**Region/gimmick.** Countryside, temperate ("Comfortable" — no heating/cooling needed). Tutorial
map, the only level restricted to a single plot. Everyone gets a permanent +10% happiness buff
("Hogsport is lovely"). No disasters. Starting cash $200,000. VIP: Health Inspector Henry Jobsworth.

**New content.** Rooms: GP's Office, General Diagnosis, Ward, Pharmacy, Staff Room, Toilets,
De-Lux Clinic. Illnesses: Grout, Clamp, Misery Guts, Bed Face, Bogwarts, Headcrabedness and the
signature visual illness Lightheadedness (De-Lux Clinic).

**Star goals.** 1★: Cure 3 Lightheadedness patients. 2★: Cure 25 patients, earn $200,000,
Hospital Level 6. 3★: Cure 30 patients, earn $400,000, Hospital Value $750,000.

**3-star strategy.** Keep rooms small (3×3 GP/diagnosis) and at Prestige level 3+; save room
templates now for reuse all game. Hire cheap 1–2-qualification staff matched to role (GP doctors
for GP's Offices, Treatment doctors for the De-Lux Clinic, Diagnosis/Ward nurses); no training
exists yet, so avoid expensive consultants. Fire extinguisher in every machine room; bins and
benches proactively. Build the De-Lux Clinic when the first Lightheadedness patient arrives, and
add a second De-Lux Clinic and second Ward for emergencies (5–7 patients each; accept them — even
partial cures pay). Hospital Value: just stay profitable month over month and don't overpay on
promotions (default raise or slightly less is fine). Stay for all 3 stars — the star rewards
(machine upgrades/items in this region) are only obtainable here, and it's fast.

**R.E.M.I.X — Hogsport** (unlock: 1★ Flottering). *Complete Wave 10* — Horde Challenge: patients
arrive in waves (10 patients, +5 per wave, 70% cure target each wave; a 10-cure streak boosts
income to 150%). Still one plot. Speed strategy: 3 GP's Offices with GP II–III doctors max; keep
the room set simple (Pharmacy ×2, Ward with 2+ Ward-trained nurses, Psychiatry, Cardiology, then
De-Lux, Pans Lab and Clown Clinic as later waves demand — Psychiatry-only illnesses like Mock
Star/Boggled Mind appear from wave 5 on). Two reception desks; train Assistants in Customer
Service for faster check-in; upgrade machines before decor; kiosks/arcades for side income.
Expect to run at a loss or on loans until wave 5–6 — that's normal.

## Level 2: Lower Bullocks

**Region/gimmick.** Countryside, temperate, no disasters. Starting cash $150,000. Introduces
buying extra plots and machine upgrades (via Bungle Technologies objectives: cure a Psychiatry
patient → Drug Mixer II/EZ-Scan II; upgrade a machine → Pans Lab). VIP: Sally Figblanket.

**New content.** Rooms: Psychiatry (diagnoses *and* treats; needs Psychiatry-qualified doctor),
Pans Lab; Cardiology unlocks at 1★. Illnesses: Mock Star, Verbal Diarrhoea, Lazy Bones,
Portishead, Pandemic, Monobrow, Potty Mouth, Freudian Lips.

**Star goals.** 1★: Hospital Level 6, own 2 buildings, cure 3 patients in Pans Lab. 2★: Hospital
Level 8, own 3 buildings, upgrade 2 machines. 3★: Hospital Level 10, cure 20 patients, 70% cure
rate, 65% hospital attractiveness.

**3-star strategy.** Easy level; the goals teach expansion. Buy the two cheapest plots ($30–50k
range) as soon as the "own buildings" goals appear — a plot counts once built out. Hire a janitor
with Mechanics for the upgrade goals and upgrade the Drug Mixer/EZ-Scan you're given. For 70%
cure rate, keep Treatment-qualified staff in treatment rooms via the Job Assignment screen and
send hopeless cases home before they die. Attractiveness 65%: plants/art in corridors, Prestige 3+
everywhere, enough bins. Psychiatry illnesses are common (druid/rock-star patient theme), so run
two Psychiatry rooms by 2★.

**R.E.M.I.X — Lower Bullocks** (unlock: 1★ Flottering). *Own 4 Plots* — Plot Challenge mode
(plots are unlocked by objectives, not money): Plot 2 = cure 30 patients in Psychiatry + 10 in
Pans Lab; Plot 3 = upgrade 3 machines; Plot 4 = shoot 20 Monobeasts. Speed strategy: build 2–3
Psychiatry rooms immediately with Psychiatry-trained doctors and market/accept everything; keep a
Mechanics janitor upgrading whatever machines you have; for Monobeasts, pick up and drop corridor
items to flush the beasts out and click-shoot them (Monobrow patients are plentiful here). (Note:
this remix's GameFAQs page was never archived; goals above are from the wiki.)

## Level 3: Flottering

**Region/gimmick.** Countryside, temperate, no disasters. Starting cash $200,000. Training level:
initial objectives (Hospital Level 4, hire 7 staff, 55% staff morale) grant the Training Licence.
VIPs: Mayor Tabitha Windsock and Health Minister Tarquin Foxbridge.

**New content.** Rooms: Training Room, Clown Clinic (unlocked by training 1 staff member).
Illnesses: Jest Infection, Mime Crisis, Lycanthropy. 1★ reward: Reception *room*.

**Star goals.** 1★: Train 3 staff, promote 3 staff. 2★: Train 6, promote 6, Hospital Level 9.
3★: Train 10, promote 10, Hospital Level 12, staff morale 75%.

**3-star strategy.** Train continuously in small batches — a hired staff member teaching a
qualification they already have is far cheaper than $10k-per-student guest trainers. Prioritise
GP for doctors, Diagnosis/Ward Management for nurses; "Fast Learner" trait now matters. Promotions
tick the goal — accept them but keep the salary slider near default. Morale 75%: staff room
prestige, send unhappy staff on breaks, don't refuse too many raises. Emergencies: Ward waves and
"Circus Incident" (Clown Clinic) — train Ward Management, keep the Dehumorifier upgraded (the
level's 2★/3★ rewards are Dehumorifier and Heart Racer upgrades). Good map to shoot 10 Monobeasts
in a row for the achievement.

**R.E.M.I.X — Flottering.** *Train 20 staff; train 3 staff in Treatment III; 70% cure rate.*
Normal diagnosis/treatment income is heavily nerfed; instead you get $8,000 per completed training
class plus Duckworth-style "Side Quests" (train a doctor/nurse/assistant/janitor, promote a staff
member). Speed strategy: hire only Students with clean traits and train nonstop — the level pays
you to. The bottleneck is Treatment III: give those three staff *exclusively* Treatment
qualifications (a non-Treatment qualification in an early slot forces ~1,475 XP instead of ~875
to reach Treatment III). Nurses level Treatment faster than doctors here (Pharmacy patients are
quick), and illness-targeted marketing works — spam Pharmacy campaigns to feed them XP. Keep rooms
at Prestige 3 and stay cheap; guest trainers are fine since training is the income.

## Level 4: Mitton University

**Region/gimmick.** Cold Region (first cold map — radiators required; watch Thermal Comfort in
every corner), no disasters. Starting cash $200,000. Research is introduced (licence: Hospital
Level 3 + upgrade a machine). Only **student** doctors and nurses can be hired — zero
qualifications, fully mouldable — and every completed training class pays a $5,000 bonus. VIP:
Augustus Lavender.

**New content.** Rooms: Research Room; Chromatherapy and Injection Room via research projects
(Fluid Analysis, X-Ray, M.E.G.A Scan are also research-unlocked here). Illnesses: Grey Anatomy,
Night Fever, Mood Poisoning, Inflated Ego, Rock Bottom, Decision Rash.

**Star goals.** 1★: Train a doctor in Research, complete the Chromatherapy research project, cure
a patient in Chromatherapy. 2★: Train 10 staff, generate 2,000 research points, cure 50 patients.
3★: Train 20 staff, generate 4,000 research points, cure 100 patients, 60% cure rate.

**3-star strategy.** Beloved map — use it as your staff factory and research base. Pick students
with positive traits only and train exactly the skills you need; batch-train 3–4 at a time and
profit from the $5k class bonuses (guest-train one teacher, then have them teach peers). Keep the
Research Room modest at first (1–2 Research doctors), expand with Servers/Super Computers later;
Research doctors + Mechanics janitors let you push machine upgrades cheaply. The 60% cure-rate bar
is low; the real work is research-point volume — park several Research III+ doctors on projects
permanently. Many players return to Mitton all game to run research. This is also the easiest map
to sweep every end-of-year award.

**R.E.M.I.X — Mitton University.** *Own 8 Plots* — plot-unlock objectives centred on training and
research: Plot 2 = place 7 Servers & Super Computers + level-5 Research Room; Plot 3 = train 2
staff each in Psychiatry, Injection Administration, Ward Management; Plot 4 = train 20 staff,
2,500 research points, 70% cure rate; Plots 5/6 = 450/800 research points; Plot 7 = train 3 staff
in Treatment II; Plot 8 = train 5 staff. Earthquakes occur here (unlike vanilla Mitton). Speed
strategy: hospital first, research second — a small Research Room early, a 4–6-seat Training Room,
$50k loan for an opening training blitz (Research, GP, Diagnosis, Ward, Psychiatry, Treatment one
each, then batches of 3–4). Do Treatment II with nurses. Don't overbuild treatment rooms before
you have staff for them; keep 1 diagnosis room per GP's Office and enable Fast Track when GPs clog.

## Level 5: Tumble

**Region/gimmick.** Cold Region — the first map with Thermal Comfort *goals*, and the first with
natural disasters (mild earthquakes that damage machines). Starting cash $200,000. No initial
objectives — straight into star goals. Star rewards are the Small Radiator (1★) and Large
Radiator (2★).

**New content.** Room: Fracture Ward (multi-illness, multiple beds/nurses like the Ward).
Illnesses: Humerus Injury, Hurty Leg, Jazz Hand, Broken Face, Cross Bones.

**Star goals.** 1★: Cure 50 patients, 50% cure rate, 60% thermal comfort. 2★: Cure 100 patients,
70% cure rate, Hospital Value $1,000,000. 3★: Cure 200 patients, 80% cure rate, Hospital Value
$2,000,000, 90% thermal comfort.

**3-star strategy.** Build a large Fracture Ward early (~7 beds carries you most of the level;
2–3 nurses per 8–10 beds) and train Ward Management; unlock Wall Monitors (Kudosh) for a treatment
boost, and Medicine Cabinets fit its tight spaces. Most emergencies are Fracture waves, with
occasional Ward and Circus Incidents. Train janitors in Maintenance — earthquakes strain every
machine at once. 90% thermal comfort means radiators in every room *and* corridor stretch; use
the heatmap overlay and the radiators you win at 1★/2★. Big pitfall: overspending early — the
Fracture Ward is expensive, so expand only as cash allows; this is the first level where you can
genuinely dig yourself into a hole.

**R.E.M.I.X — Tumble.** *Complete Wave 1* — a single super-horde of **100 patients, 75% target
cure rate**, mostly Fracture Ward illnesses (Humerus Injury ~25%, Hurty Leg ~20%, Broken Face
~20%, Cross Bones ~15%, plus some Shattered). You get ~100 days' prep, R.E.M.I.X covers most
running costs, and you start with 8 untrained doctors, 12 untrained nurses, 5 assistants, 6
janitors and several training rooms. Speed strategy: fire bad-trait staff immediately, then
guest-train everything at once (author's opening class: 8 nurses Ward Management, 4 nurses
Diagnostics, 6 doctors GP, 4 doctors Radiology, janitor Mechanics II, 4 assistants Customer
Service) across ~6 temporary training rooms — sell most after the first round. Build two Fracture
Wards (5-bed + 8-bed, 2 nurses each, Wall Monitors), 2 M.E.G.A Scans + 1 X-Ray + 2 General
Diagnosis, 4–6 GP's Offices, 4 reception pods, radiators everywhere, hygiene items to prevent
vomit cascades. Drop Fast Track threshold to 85%, lock staff to rooms via Job Assignment, take
both loans (costs are subsidised), skip Prestige-4 decor — spend on training and machine upgrades
instead. Add De-Lux/Pharmacy/Pans Lab plus a third Fracture Ward and small Ward mid-wave as
requested. Clearing the wave first try lands ~May of Year 2.

## Level 6: Flemington

**Region/gimmick.** Cold Region, wealthy town — no disasters. Starting cash $250,000. Marketing
introduced: research Pest Control + diagnose an Animal Magnetism patient to earn the Marketing
Licence and Marketing Room. Café unlocks at 1★. VIPs: Jasmine Odyssey, Roderick Cushion.
Clearing 1★ here also unlocks most DLC maps.

**New content.** Rooms: Marketing Room, Pest Control (research). Illnesses: Animal Magnetism,
Boggled Mind, Litter Bug, and **Shattered** — one of the hardest cures in the game (Fracture Ward
staff at qualification III+ and extra Wall Monitors, or it will tank your cure rate).

**Star goals.** 1★: Hospital Value $1,000,000, 70% reputation, cure 10 Animal Magnetism patients,
launch a marketing campaign. 2★: Value $2,000,000, 80% reputation, cure 20 in Fracture Ward,
launch 2 campaigns. 3★: Value $5,000,000, 90% reputation, cure 30 in Injection Room, launch 3
campaigns.

**3-star strategy.** The patients are rich: raise diagnosis/treatment prices 10–20% early — you
need the margin for the $5M value goal, which is really "run a big profit month after month".
Marketing campaigns count on *launch*, so short 3-month campaigns tick goals cheaply and also
drive the reputation targets; train an Assistant in Marketing. Cure-30-in-Injection-Room is the
slow one: the Injection Room (researched back in Mitton) treats many minor illnesses — build two
and steer patients there. Segregate GP/diagnosis, treatment, and admin blocks; keep the helipad
near treatment for emergencies (mostly Fracture waves).

**R.E.M.I.X — Flemington.** *Get balance to $350,000; conduct 25 months of marketing (total);
70% cure rate.* Only one patient per treatment room shows up organically — you must attract the
rest with illness marketing, while timed Side Quests ("cure 3–5 of illness X", "start 3
campaigns", level-5 Marketing Room) pay $25k–$70k. Earthquakes occur here. Speed strategy: keep
one or more small (3×4) Marketing Rooms running *constantly* — 25 months is the hard clock, so
never let campaigns idle; keep $15k banked so a campaign is always launchable; cheap staff
campaigns ($7k/3 months for doctor/nurse/janitor recruitment) also count toward the 25 months and
fix this level's nasty hiring pool (random consultants, "Wants more money" traits — grab any
cheap trainable intern you see). Expect the treatment-room tech tree to roll out roughly Pharmacy
→ Ward → Psychiatry → De-Lux → Fracture Ward → Injection → Pest Control → Colourizer → Pans Lab →
Clown Clinic; upgrade each machine to level II on build. Raise prices ~20% once reputation is
solid; buy the cheap plots (Plot 6 first). Realistic clear: Year 5.

## Level 7: Smogley

**Region/gimmick.** Industrial Region, cold, polluted; no natural disasters. Starting cash
$250,000. A teaching hospital focused on Surgery — the star goals are training- and surgery-
gated. VIP: Sophie Nova. (The task brief's "research-locked treatments + dirty" is only half
right: Smogley's Head Office is a research project and the town is grimy, but the defining
mechanics are training volume and Surgery, per both the guide and the wiki.)

**New content.** Rooms: Operating Theatre (Surgery — needs a Surgery-qualified doctor *plus* a
nurse), Head Office (research project). Illnesses: Turtle Head (Head Office), Spinal Bap, Floppy
Discs, Pipe Organs (all Surgery).

**Star goals.** 1★: Train 10 staff, cure 15 in Surgery, cure 5 Turtle Head. 2★: Train 20 staff,
cure 30 in Surgery, 75% cure rate, Hospital Level 12. 3★: Train 30 staff, cure 50 in Surgery,
90% cure rate, Hospital Level 16.

**3-star strategy.** Surgery is the profit engine and the goal — run multiple well-equipped
Operating Theatres (Kudosh extras help) and push one doctor toward Surgery V (Steam achievement
"Cut Above"). Expect Turtle Head and Surgery emergency waves. The 90% cure rate comes organically
from upgraded machines plus deeply trained staff — no patient-purge needed. Keep 1:1 GP-to-
diagnosis room ratio and audit the Job Assignment screen: the guide's own failing-1-star case
study traces its debt to a consultant surgeon idling in a GP's Office, oversized staff rooms, and
diagnosis/treatment rooms scattered across the wrong plots. Consolidate diagnosis near GPs,
treatment in a back block, and shrink over-large rooms; that alone flipped the case-study
hospital back into profit. Nothing new unlocks at 2★/3★, but the level is good Surgery training
for the rest of the game.

## Level 8: Melt Downs

**Region/gimmick.** Industrial Region, cold; an abandoned hospital by the power plant. Starting
cash $250,000, reputation 40%. Plots are full of rubble and wrecked machines that janitors must
clear before use; disasters: **electrical storms** (new) and earthquakes. Awkward narrow-corridor
buildings — "if it fits, it fits" beats standard templates here.

**New content.** Rooms (both research projects): DNA Lab (treats *and* is a powerful diagnosis
room; needs a Genetics-qualified doctor), Shock Clinic. Illnesses: Shock Horror, Flumps, Denim
Genes, Jumbo DNA, Emperor Complex. 1★ reward: Toxic-Waste Bin (uniquely doesn't count as negative
environment).

**Star goals.** 1★: 1,000 research points, upgrade 5 machines, cure 5 in Shock Clinic, cure 10
in DNA Lab. 2★: Cure 20 in DNA Lab, upgrade 10 machines, Hospital Level 10, 75% cure rate.
3★: Cure 40 in DNA Lab, upgrade 15 machines, Hospital Level 15, 90% cure rate.

**3-star strategy.** Research the DNA Lab first (Shock Clinic patients are scarce); guest-train a
student doctor in Genetics immediately. DNA Labs pay extremely well but clog with diagnosis
traffic — set one or two labs to "Treatment only" in the room panel, and upgrade the Healixer
aggressively because DNA patients are among the game's hardest cures. Janitors are the hidden
MVPs: 3–4 early (a dozen late), Mechanics for the 15 machine upgrades, Maintenance + Stamina for
storm damage. Pace the upgrade goals — upgrade exactly 5, then 10, then 15. Keep emergency-prone
rooms (DNA Lab) near the helipad. This is the first map big enough to run two separate
GP/diagnosis intake areas feeding one treatment core — practice it; the late game demands it.

## Level 9: Duckworth-upon-Bilge

**Region/gimmick.** Industrial Region, cold; no disasters. A **public hospital**: diagnosis and
treatment are free, so nearly all income comes from rolling ministry targets ("run a marketing
campaign", "diagnose 5 patients", "promote a staff member", "water the plants"...). Starts at
Hospital Level 4 with 31% reputation and $250,000. Epidemics debut here (Abominable Curse).
Widely rated one of the hardest levels in the game.

**New content.** Room: Resolution Lab (research project). Illness: 8-bitten.

**Star goals.** 1★: Hospital Level 8, 60% staff morale, 60% reputation, cure 5 8-bitten patients.
2★: Level 12, 75% morale, 75% reputation, cure 100 patients. 3★: Level 16, 90% morale, 90%
reputation, cure 200 patients.

**3-star strategy.** Forget profit — chase targets. If a target is unworkable, X it out and take
the replacement. Build a Marketing Room early: many targets are "launch a campaign" (launching,
not finishing, pays) and marketing props up the reputation goals. Bank staff promotions ("Not
now") and cash them in when the promote-a-staff-member target appears. Janitors complete a huge
share of targets (plants, toilets, vending restocks, attractiveness) — hire several, train
Stamina. Research-for-money gives reliable $20k top-ups; emergencies, VIP visits and cured
epidemics also pay. Loans are fine — target income usually repays them fast. The last wall is 90%
staff morale: pay everyone above average *late* (not from the start), sweep the staff list and
force breaks on anyone unhappy, keep training/promotions flowing, decorate rooms for the buff.
Treat staff as replaceable; salaries are your only real cost lever. Pause often and triage.

## Level 10: Sweaty Palms

**Region/gimmick.** Tropical Region — **hot** climate (Air Con and Small Air Con units, unlocked
with Kudosh, replace radiators; janitors maintain them). Disasters: earthquakes and **volcanic
eruptions** (lava scorches random tiles and sets machines alight — extinguishers are single-use,
stock double the usual amount, including corridors). Epidemics continue. Starting cash $250,000.

**New content.** Room: Cryptology. Illness: Premature Mummification (the base game's final clinic
illness).

**Star goals.** 1★: Cure 15 Premature Mummification patients, 60% cure rate, Hospital Value
$750,000, 60% thermal comfort. 2★: Cure 100 patients, 75% cure rate, Value $2,000,000, 75%
thermal comfort. 3★: Cure 200 patients, 90% cure rate, Value $5,000,000, 90% thermal comfort.

**3-star strategy.** Build Cryptology early and market Premature Mummification for the 1★ goal
(mummies also headline an emergency type here). Thermal comfort 90% works like Tumble in reverse —
Air Cons everywhere, checked against the temperature overlay. Keep janitor counts high and
Maintenance-trained: eruptions + quakes + Air Con upkeep is a triple load, and exploded machines
(five-figure replacements) are how this map bankrupts you. The guide author restarted this level
after 1★ due to bloated salaries, exploded machines, GP offices outpacing diagnosis rooms, and a
bad layout — balance GP:diagnosis 1:1 and audit wages. Vaccinate epidemics promptly for ~$15k
bonuses. Value $5M = sustained monthly profit; expand across the cheap plots steadily.

## Level 11: Grockle Bay

**Region/gimmick.** Tropical Region, hot; tourists bring exotic illnesses and, per the wiki, this
is where the hot climate formally debuts. Disasters: earthquakes; epidemic: Jogger's Ripple
(hard to spot — the tell is a subtle gait). Awkward, beautiful plots split by water and bridges
with cramped 4×4 pads at plot entrances. Starting cash $250,000. VIP: Agatha Sphere. (The task
brief's "money/VIPs" framing: the goals are actually value/attractiveness/reputation — a
money-and-image map rather than a VIP mechanic.)

**New content.** Room: Recurvery Room (researched in Sweaty Palms, but Cubism patients — its
customers — effectively arrive here). Illnesses: Cubism, Pudding Blood, Heart Throb.

**Star goals.** 1★: Hospital Value $750,000, 60% attractiveness, cure 10 Cubism patients. 2★:
Value $2,000,000, 70% attractiveness, 75% reputation, 75% cure rate. 3★: Value $4,000,000, 80%
attractiveness, 90% reputation, 90% cure rate.

**3-star strategy.** Patient pressure spikes early — don't hesitate to send stuck patients home
or fast-track to treatment before you can expand. Attractiveness is a *tracked goal*: Prestige 3+
rooms, and blanket the long bridge corridors with maintenance-free decor (Palm Tree/Sonic Tree,
or Yucca/Cactus if cheap; spam artwork in a pinch) — regular plants wither faster than janitors
can water on a map this spread out. Use the 4×4 entrance pads for Air Cons, vending, decor and
entertainment rather than squeezing rooms in. Expect heavy Fracture, Psychiatry and Shock Horror
traffic plus Pharmacy/Shock/Psychiatry/DNA emergencies; DNA Lab is also Cubism's best diagnosis
room. Market Cubism once a Treatment-trained doctor and an upgraded Recurvery machine are ready.
The author finished with ~24 Maintenance/Stamina janitors for quakes and Air Cons. When arrival
queues hit 30–40, relocate reception deeper into the map (Plot 5) and re-centre the hospital
around it.

## Level 12: Blighton

**Region/gimmick.** Tropical Region, hot; "riddled with contagious diseases". Constant epidemics
(both Abominable Curse and Jogger's Ripple) plus earthquakes; hygiene and vaccination are star
goals. Small plots force compact builds. Starting cash $250,000. No new rooms; last new base-game
illness: Leopard Skin.

**Star goals.** 1★: Hospital Level 10, 70% hygiene rating, vaccinate 30 infected people, 90%
thermal comfort. 2★: Level 14, 80% hygiene, vaccinate 45, 75% cure rate. 3★: Level 18, 90%
hygiene, vaccinate 60, 90% cure rate.

**3-star strategy.** Run it like a pandemic response: hand sanitisers everywhere (inside every
room), big toilet blocks with sinks and hand dryers, hire Hygienic staff and reject Unhygienic
ones. Design *against* crowds — keep 2-tile corridors but avoid big open congregation spaces; a
compact "factory line" flow limits epidemic spread. You must actually vaccinate (30/45/60), so
chase every outbreak early — vaccinations pay well — but late-level epidemics spread so fast
they're sometimes unwinnable; you can cancel an epidemic from its info panel for a reputation hit,
so keep a Marketing Room running to repair reputation. Hospital Level 18 is the highest levelling
goal so far: keep hiring and adding rooms. Note 90% *thermal comfort* is required at 1★ on a hot
map — saturate with Air Cons immediately.

## Level 13: Rotting Hill

**Region/gimmick.** Urban Region, cold. A failing inner-city hospital: you start **−$10,000 in
debt**, 17% reputation, Hospital Level 3, and every patient and staff member carries a permanent
−10% happiness trait ("Rotting Hill is horrible"). No disasters, but frequent epidemics and Ward
emergencies. No new rooms/illnesses. One of the game's hardest maps; the guide author needed four
attempts on his first playthrough.

**Star goals.** 1★: Cure 100 patients, 70% reputation, 70% staff morale, Hospital Value
$1,000,000. 2★: Cure 200, 80%/80%, $2,000,000. 3★: Cure 500, **95% reputation, 95% staff
morale**, $5,000,000.

**3-star strategy.** Fight the map debuff structurally: every room to Prestige 4 (the buff
offsets the −10%), train Emotional Happiness as a third/fourth qualification, and load corridors
with happiness items (arcades, newsstands, gift shops). Run a Duckworth-style disposable-staff
economy: staff beyond their third qualification cost more than they return — trim payroll, keep
just a few Training-Masterclass teachers to churn out replacements, and never hire Wants More
Money / Unhygienic / Nasty / Evil candidates. Skip Research doctors entirely (nothing left to
research; they're the most expensive staff) and skip the Café (space is better as GPs/diagnosis).
Patients "refuse to pay" more here, so don't over-raise prices — win on throughput; Wards plateau
around 8–10 beds with 3–4 nurses. Marketing is vital early (the hiring pool is random trash) and
late (attract expensively-treated illnesses). Save the 95% morale push for last: temporarily
overpay everyone, sweep the staff list sending the unhappy on breaks — accept it may wreck
long-term finances after the star lands. Check janitors often; the sprawling layout exhausts them.

## Level 14: Pelican Wharf

**Region/gimmick.** Urban Region, cold, high-tech district. **No natural disasters** — the threat
is rival CEO **Jumbo McNally**, the final VIP, who visits to sabotage your machines. Goals centre
on research and machine upgrades. One starting plot; surrounding real estate is expensive.
Starting cash $300,000, reputation 40%. No new rooms or illnesses.

**Star goals.** 1★: Cure 100 patients, 60% cure rate, train 3 staff in Research III, upgrade 15
machines. 2★: Cure 200, 75% cure rate, train 2 staff in Research IV, upgrade 15 machines. 3★:
Cure 500, 90% cure rate, train 1 staff member to Research V, upgrade 15 machines.

**3-star strategy.** Critical trap: the training goals count only staff trained **after** the
relevant star — don't pre-train Research IV/V or the credit is wasted (the guide author had to
train a second Research V doctor because his first was trained too early). Likewise each star
wants 15 *new* machine upgrades — keep a stable of Mechanics-heavy janitors and a research team
permanently on upgrade projects, and pace upgrades so you always have 15 available to do. Patient
volume is enormous: build multiple mirrored diagnosis+treatment complexes ("copies of the same
basic hospital") rather than one centre, and mind walking distances — on a map this size patients
die in transit if rooms are badly placed. The author built roughly counter-clockwise from one
side. Otherwise no gimmicks: efficiency, deep training, and upgrades are the whole level.

## Level 15: Croquembouche

**Region/gimmick.** Urban Region, cold. The finale: the busiest patient flow in the game, every
base-game illness and treatment room, earthquakes, epidemics, frequent VIP visits of all kinds,
frequent (often unreasonable) emergencies, and the game's most expensive plots ($130,000 for the
large park plots). You begin on a distinctive cross-shaped plot at roughly Hospital Level 6
equivalent pressure. Starting cash $300,000. Contrary to the "one-plot challenge" guess, there
are many plots — the constraint is cost and relentless patient pressure, not plot count.

**Star goals.** 1★: Cure 250 patients, 70% cure rate, 70% reputation, Hospital Value $2,500,000.
2★: Cure 500, 80%, 80%, $5,000,000. 3★: Cure 1,000, 90%, 90%, $10,000,000.

**3-star strategy.** BUILD, BUILD, BUILD: add a room whenever any queue hits ~6. Ignore the cross
shape — pick one arm as your front door and treat the other three doors as walls until expansion
forces the issue; ≤4 receptionists until 1★. Opening core: Ward, Pharmacy, Psychiatry, De-Lux,
then a rapid rollout of Clown Clinic, Pans Lab, Pest Control, Chromatherapy, Shock Clinic,
Fracture Ward, Injection Room; hold DNA Lab and Surgery until staffing is genuinely ready. Buy
the central $130k plots before cheaper distant ones — walking distance kills. Take multiple loans
freely; patient volume repays them. **Reject emergencies you aren't built for** — they draw from
all illnesses and saying no is fine. Two staff rooms from the start, energy drinks/coffee in all
of them; later, near-nonstop training (the author ran seven training rooms and finished with ~121
staff: 50+ doctors, ~30 nurses, 15 assistants, 25 janitors — roughly 8 good janitors per star,
3–4 with Mechanics). Add a second reception area (with its own GP/diagnosis feed) around Hospital
Level 14–16. Marketing runs permanently to keep the hiring pool alive; research doctors are worth
keeping here for $20k project payouts. $10M value = sustained profit: prioritise getting patients
*through* diagnosis to treatment over everything else.
