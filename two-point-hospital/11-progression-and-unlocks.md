# Two Point Hospital — Progression and Unlocks

## Career structure: Two Point County

The base campaign spans **15 hospitals in 5 regions** ("Worlds" 1–5 on the wiki): **Countryside, Cold, Industrial, Beach** (some guides call it Tropical), and **Urban**. Each region has its own climate and gimmicks, and each hospital has its own 1/2/3-star objective lists. **Reaching 1 star is always what unlocks the next hospital(s)**; 2 and 3 stars are optional (they pay cash/Kudosh, unlock items, and feed career goals/achievements like "earn 45 gold stars"). Hospitals persist — you can leave at 1 star and return later.

DLC regions bolt another 7 regions (3 hospitals each) onto the same map, for **36 hospitals across 12 regions** with everything installed.

Pattern: each region is a pair of parallel hospitals gated behind the previous one, then a capstone third hospital requiring 1 star in both (Worlds 2–5); World 1 is strictly linear.

### What each base hospital introduces

Rooms/treatments/features debut on specific maps and are then available (or researchable) everywhere after:

- **Hogsport** — tutorial. Reception desk, GP's Office, General Diagnosis, Pharmacy, Ward, Staff Room, Toilets; first visual "clinic" illness **Lightheadedness** and its **De-Lux Clinic**. VIP: Henry Jobsworth. Star objectives (for reference): ★1 cure 3 Lightheadedness; ★2 cure 25 patients, earn $200k, hospital level 6; ★3 cure 30, earn $400k, hospital value $750k.
- **Lower Bullocks** — **Psychiatry** (diagnosis+treatment), **Cardiology** (at ★1), **Pans Lab** for the new clinic illness **Pandemic**; machine-upgrade rewards; VIP Sally Figblanket.
- **Flottering** — **Training room** (objectives revolve around it), **Reception room** unlock at ★1, **Clown Clinic** for **Jest Infection**. VIPs: Mayor Tabitha Windsock, Health Minister Tarquin Foxbridge.
- **Mitton University** — first **cold** map (radiators); introduces **Research room**; hire pool is students only, forcing training; **Chromatherapy** for **Grey Anatomy**; Fluid Analysis, X-Ray, M.E.G.A Scan become researchable. VIP Augustus Lavender.
- **Tumble** — **Fracture Ward** (multi-illness); first **earthquake** disasters; no initial objectives, straight to star goals.
- **Flemington** — **Marketing room** (campaigns), **Café** at ★1, **Pest Control** for **Animal Magnetism**. Completing Flemington ★1 opens World 3 (and is the trigger for most DLC map pins). VIPs: Jasmine Odyssey, Roderick Cushion.
- **Smogley** — **Surgery** (multi-illness), **Head Office** for **Turtle Head**, **Shock Clinic** for **Shock Horror**. VIP Sophie Nova.
- **Melt Downs** — **DNA Lab** (treatment + strong diagnosis), electrical-storm disaster, rubble-clearing plots.
- **Duckworth-upon-Bilge** — target/public-funding economy (free care, income from meeting ministry targets); first **epidemics** (Abominable Curse); **Resolution Lab** for **8-bitten**.
- **Grockle Bay** — first **hot** map (air-con), Jogger's Ripple epidemic, **Recurvery Room** for **Cubism**. VIP Agatha Sphere.
- **Sweaty Palms** — volcanic eruptions; **Cryptology** for **Premature Mummification** (the base game's last clinic illness).
- **Blighton** — hygiene/vaccination-focused objectives; epidemics intensify.
- **Pelican Wharf** — research/upgrade emphasis; saboteur VIP Jumbo McNally debuts.
- **Rotting Hill** — mastery test of reputation and staff morale under a permanent map-wide "−10% happiness" feeling.
- **Croquembouche** — the finale: maximum patient volume, needs every treatment room, disasters + epidemics + all VIPs.

## DLC expansions

Seven story DLCs each add a **3-hospital region**, new illnesses (many visual), new cure machines/rooms, items, music and radio lines. Unlocks (PC): most DLC regions' map pins appear early and can be entered after **Flemington ★1**, but the games advise waiting — their illness rosters lean on Surgery and the DNA Lab, so they "unlock naturally" around **Duckworth-upon-Bilge ★1**. ### Free updates

- **Sandbox mode** (Oct 2018) — freeplay on any map with custom cash/Kudosh/arrival-rate/illness settings and event toggles; later expanded with objectives presets and Steam Workshop hospital sharing.
- **Interior Designer + Steam Workshop** (Feb 2019, PC) — custom wall/floor/item skins and sharing.
- **The Superbug Initiative** (Apr 2019) — co-op community research projects with exclusive item rewards (e.g. Brain Chair); also a Kudosh source.
- **R.E.M.I.X** (Update 1.18, Feb 2020) and **R.E.M.I.X v2** (1.20, May 2020) — wave-based remix versions of Hogsport, Lower Bullocks, Flottering, then Mitton University, Tumble, Flemington; each grants one R.E.M.I.X star (e.g. Hogsport: clear wave 10 for $10,000 + K500). Unlocked from Flottering ★1.
- **Room Templates** (Update 1.21) — save/copy-paste whole room layouts across all your hospitals.
- Consoles later received bundled "Healthcheck"/Hospital Pass content updates mirroring PC features.

## Kudosh economy

### Earning (typical amounts)

| Source | Typical payout |
|---|---|
| Career Goals (lifetime milestones: cure 100/1k/10k/50k patients, promote 100 of a staff type, train 20/50 staff, upgrade 5/25/100 machines, 10/50 marketing campaigns, org value $10M/$20M/$50M, 15/30/45 stars, capture 100 ghosts, etc.) | K100–K1,000 each |
| Star ratings (per star, scales with map) | ~K100–K500 |
| Hospital ad-hoc objectives / staff & patient challenges (timed "cure N in X days" style) | varies, small-to-mid |
| Award Ceremony win | K5 per award |
| VIP good report | ~K10–K20 |
| Emergency success / perfect | K10 / K15 |
| Epidemic containment | ~K10–K15 (sources vary) |
| Research "Generate Kudosh" project | repeatable trickle |
| Superbug Initiative nodes/completions | varies |

Kudosh is account/organisation-wide and there's effectively no shortage late-game; each item is a **one-time unlock** that then becomes buyable with cash in every hospital (Sandbox can be set to "all items unlocked" instead).

### Spending priorities (community consensus, GameFAQs guide)

First purchases that pay for themselves:

1. **Medicine Cabinet** — +diagnosis power in GP's Offices (arguably the single best unlock).
2. **Hand Sanitiser** — cheap hygiene maintenance.
3. **Food Pyramid Poster** (and other cheap posters: Eye Test, Rorschach) — bargain room-prestige boosts.
4. **Arcade Machine** — entertainment that *earns* money.
5. **Energy Drinks Machine** — staff-room stamina.
6. **Diagnosis Bookcase / Treatment Bookcase** — +2% scores in Psychiatry.
7. **Big Bin** — fewer janitor trips.
8. **Coffee Maker** — staff micro-breaks between patients.
9. **Gold Star Award** — the classic prestige-5 "spam item".
10. Then: Luxury/Amusing snack machines, Sweet Dispenser, Laxative Drinks Machine (health restore, Pebberley), Yucca (low-maintenance plant), Research Monitors, Operation Monitor.

Skip most purely decorative unlocks until the above are owned.

## Milestone rewards worth knowing

- **Star-count career goals:** 15 stars → K500; 30 stars → K750; 45 stars (all base hospitals 3-starred) → K1,000; matching Steam achievements at 15/30/45 gold stars ("County-Wide", "Inter-continental", "Pointy Mountain G.O.A.T").
- **Cure milestones:** 100/1,000/10,000/50,000 cures (K100→K1,000); achievements at 1 and 1,000 cures.
- **Wealth milestones:** earn $1M/$10M/$100M; organisation value $10M/$20M/$50M (achievement "JUMBO" at $50M value).
- Others: level-10/level-20 hospital achievements, 100 ghost captures ("I. See. You." + K200 career goal), 25 machine upgrades, 50 research projects, 50 marketing campaigns, world-class (V-qualified) specialist trainings, winning every award in one hospital.

Beyond these, the achievement list is mostly incidental play ("shoot a Monobeast", "injure someone with an exploding machine") and not needed for progression.
