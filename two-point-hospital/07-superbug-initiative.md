# The Superbug Initiative (Community / Co-op Research)

The Superbug Initiative is Two Point Hospital's free multiplayer layer, released **16 April 2019** (PC; later on consoles). It is unlocked by reaching **Star Level 1 in Mitton University**, after which the in-fiction sponsor "Bungle Technologies" (CEO Sophie Nova) invites your organisation onto the **Superbug Network** — a screen accessed from the superbug icon at the top-right of the UI.

## How it works

- Each project is a **branching tree of research nodes**. Every node is a task performed in your own hospitals (cure N patients, train N staff, water plants, upgrade machines...). A node completes when the **requisite number of participating players** have each done its task; the group then advances along the branches toward **victory nodes** (marked with a gold star). When all victory nodes are done, the project completes and **all participants receive the rewards** (host and guests alike).
- **One global project and up to three local projects** can be active at a time on the network screen (the GameFAQs guide describes this as "up to four separate projects"). You can only have **one Superbug goal (node) active at a time** — you must abandon your current node to work on a node from another project.
- Progress updates live; players do not need to be online simultaneously. Realistically a friend-group local project takes days to a week of casual play; globals ran for weeks.
- Tasks can require **specific hospitals** (e.g. illness-specific cures only occur on certain maps), so a mature save with several functioning hospitals is a practical prerequisite.
- Joining: the host invites platform friends (Steam friends list on PC); invitations arrive via the mail icon on the network screen. Completed local projects can be **replayed** to help friends and re-farm Kudosh.

### Node task pool (local project trees are randomly generated from this pool)

| Category | Example nodes | Hospital constraint |
|---|---|---|
| Curing | Cure 10 patients; Cure 5 patients in a row; Cure 5 in Pharmacy / Psychiatry / Ward / Surgery / Injection Room / DNA Lab; Cure 3–5 of a named illness (Lightheadedness, Pandemic, Jest Infection, Grey Anatomy, Night Fever, Shock Horror, Cubism, 8-bitten, Animal Magnetism, Denim Genes, Premature Mummification, Touch of Midas, Leopard Skin, Emperor Complex...) | Illness-specific nodes tie to the maps where the illness appears (e.g. Touch of Midas → Grockle Bay/Blighton; Shock Horror → Melt Downs; 8-bitten → Duckworth-upon-Bilge) |
| Timed | Cure 10 patients within 90 days; Earn $200,000 within 90 days; Generate 1,000 RP within 120 days | Any hospital |
| Staffing | Train 3 staff / 3 doctors / 3 nurses; Promote 3 staff | Any hospital |
| Miscellaneous | Water 5 plants; Capture 3 ghosts (Roquefort Castle); Earn $200,000; Earn K50; Generate 1,000 Research Points; Upgrade 1 or 3 machines | Mostly any hospital |

Because trees are random, hosts are advised to be able to complete *any* of these tasks before starting the bigger projects. Node counts grow with project size (per TechRaptor: Hair Removal ~8–10 nodes up to Learning Machine Learning ~24–28), and each completed node/challenge pays a small Kudosh reward (~K10, guide-reported).

## Local (team) projects — full list

Five host-run projects, unlocked **sequentially** (completing one unlocks the next). Rewards go to every participant.

| # | Project | Participants | Rewards | Reward item effect |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Hair Removal | 1–3 (soloable) | **Browbeater** + K100 | Corridor device that lures/removes Monobeasts ($2,500) |
| 2 | Love Sick | 2–5 | **Love Statue** + K200 | Attractiveness statue ($8,000) |
| 3 | Dead Weight | 2–5 | **Ectovat** + K300 | Repurposes ghosts into a staff energy source ($4,000) |
| 4 | Cheese on the Chalkboard | 3–5 | **Golden Gubbins** + K400 | Attractiveness plinth ($10,000) |
| 5 | Learning Machine Learning | 4–5 | **Deep Thing 1 + Deep Thing 2** + K500 | Deep Thing 1: +2% Research / +3% Diagnosis power ($25,000); Deep Thing 2: +3% Research / +3% Treatment power ($30,000) — the only functionally significant Superbug rewards |

## Global projects — full list

Community-wide, fixed research trees, presented to all players with a time window; later nodes sometimes needed thousands of contributors. On completion, rewards became claimable by **everyone** (participant or not) until the next global began.

| The Gyro-Nut (Beta) | 17–25 Apr 2019 | 50–220 players/node | (beta run of the below) |
| The Gyro-Nut | 30 Apr – 21 May 2019 | 300–3,500 players/node | Giant Pipette, Radiation Box, Giggle Pump, Brain Chair, Hologram |
| Meditations on Mortality | 3 Jun – 7 Jul 2019 | – | Thumbs-Up Duck, The Statue |
| Creature Comfort | 30 Aug – 9 Sep 2019 | – | Aquatic Sphere, Insect Ranch, Critter Coop |
| Nice Smelling Research | 26 Nov – 8 Dec 2019 | – | Jasmine Bed, Nice Smelling Sanitiser, Jasmine EP (2 music tracks), Jasmine Statue, Gold Record |
| Healthy Appetite | 15 May – 3 Jun 2020 | – | Savoury Platter, Pudding Platter, Pickle Platter |
| Fruit Loot | 26 Jun – 4 Jul 2020 | – | Tomato Clock, Banana Phone, Apple Chair |
| A Project of Ice & Fire | 15 Dec 2020 – 1 Jan 2021 | – | Ice Fountain, Fire Fountain |

No new global project has been run since **A Project of Ice & Fire** (Dec 2020/early 2021). The global reward items were later **reissued through the Hospital Pass**, and the Ice & Fire rewards remain claimable directly from the Superbug menu — so latecomers are no longer locked out (early on, missed globals were unobtainable).

Notable reward details (from the GameFAQs items list): Brain Chair gives a "Brain Boost" moodlet; Giggle Pump gives "The Giggles"; Radiation Box/Hologram/Giant Pipette give +1% Diagnosis power and prestige; Jasmine Bed is a Ward bed that cures and diagnoses; Nice Smelling Sanitiser boosts hygiene and smells nice; the platters reduce staff hunger.

**On the "golden toilet":** no golden toilet appears in any Superbug reward list (wiki or GameFAQs). The gold-themed Superbug rewards are the **Golden Gubbins** (local project 4) and the **Gold Record** (Nice Smelling Research). Treat any "golden toilet" claim as a mix-up (likely with another Two Point game or a Kudosh item).

## Strategy

- **Do Hair Removal first** — it's soloable, unlocks the chain and the Icebreaker achievement.
- **Priority target: Learning Machine Learning**, for Deep Thing 1 & 2 — the only Superbug items with real gameplay power (research/diagnosis/treatment boosts for your research hub). It needs 4–5 committed players; recruit via the Two Point Studios Discord or the game's Steam forum if your friends list is thin.
- **Kudosh farming:** completed projects are replayable and each node pays Kudosh; guides rate **Love Sick** (2 players, ~10–20 min with active partners) and **Dead Weight** as the best Kudosh-per-time loops.
- **Prepare a versatile save** before hosting big projects: since node trees are random, you want 3-starred access to hospitals covering ghost capture (Roquefort Castle with Bigfoot DLC nodes), the named illnesses, high patient throughput for "cure X in a row/within 90 days", plus a stocked Training Room and spare machines to upgrade. Cheap upgradable machines and pre-queued trainees let you clear staffing/upgrade nodes instantly.
- Keep one hospital rich enough to trivially hit "Earn $200,000 (within 90 days)" money nodes.

## Online status

- The Superbug Initiative requires a connection to Two Point's online service; players have long reported intermittent "Superbug connection lost" errors (restarting the game is the usual fix), and the developers shipped connection fixes, including a beta-branch fix discussed on Steam as recently as 2025.
- As of this writing (researched July 2026) there is **no announced shutdown** of the Superbug servers that could be found; local project co-op still functions per community reports, but **no new global projects have run since 2020–21**, and the mode is effectively in maintenance mode. Treat live availability as uncertain — verify in-game.
